## 2. About the Course

### Objectives

By the end of this course you will:

1. Have a **global overview of big data** and the key concepts behind it.
2. Understand the **benefits of big data** and how companies use it to improve their business.
3. **Use some of the main technologies** in the big data ecosystem and understand what they are used for.
4. Study the most common **big data architectures and platforms** used by companies.
5. Establish the **foundations for the courses** coming up next term.
6. Take your **first steps with Apache Spark**, the industry-leading framework for big data analytics.
7. Become familiar with the **tools used by data engineers and data scientists**.
8. Be encouraged to pursue your **own projects and initiatives** through exercises and labs.

### Bibliography

**Course bibliography**

- Balamurugan Balusamy, Nandhini Abirami R, Seifedine Kadry and Amir H. Gandomi. ***Big Data: Concepts, Technology, and Architecture.*** 1st edition. Wiley, 2021.
- Bill Chambers and Matei Zaharia. ***Spark: The Definitive Guide.*** 1st edition. O'Reilly, 2018. *Written for Spark 2.x; the core concepts still apply, though some APIs have changed in Spark 3 and 4.*

**Newer recommended reading**

- Jules S. Damji, Brooke Wenig, Tathagata Das and Denny Lee. ***Learning Spark: Lightning-Fast Data Analytics.*** 2nd edition. O'Reilly, 2020. *Covers Spark 3.0, Structured Streaming and Delta Lake.*
- Joe Reis and Matt Housley. ***Fundamentals of Data Engineering.*** O'Reilly, 2022. *The data engineering lifecycle and modern data architectures.*
- Martin Kleppmann and Chris Riccomini. ***Designing Data-Intensive Applications.*** 2nd edition. O'Reilly, 2026. *The reference on how modern data systems work under the hood.*

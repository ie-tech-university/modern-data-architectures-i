About the course

How the course works

Objectives

By the end of this course you will:

  1. Have a global overview of big data and the key concepts behind it.
  2. Understand the benefits of big data and how companies use it to improve their business.
  3. Use some of the main technologies in the big data ecosystem and understand what they are used for.
  4. Study the most common big data architectures and platforms used by companies.

Objectives (continued)

  1. Establish the foundations for the courses coming up next term.
  2. Take your first steps with Apache Spark, the industry-leading framework for big data analytics.
  3. Become familiar with the tools used by data engineers and data scientists.
  4. Be encouraged to pursue your own projects and initiatives through exercises and labs.

Materials

All the materials you need to follow and pass the course will be provided in advance on Blackboard (Additional Documentation section).

The bibliography is optional. It is aimed at students who want to explore the topics covered in class in more depth.

Discussion board (forum)

Every session has its own discussion board on Blackboard to encourage participation.

  1. Post in the thread most related to your topic.
  2. Read the existing posts before posting: your question may already be answered.
  3. Questions on the discussion board are preferred over questions by email.
